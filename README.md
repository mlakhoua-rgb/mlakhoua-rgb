## Engineering principles

The repositories published here demonstrate how I approach AI-native engineering:

* **AI accelerates execution; humans retain accountability**
* **Security and governance are architectural concerns**
* **Missing or invalid configuration must fail closed**
* **Every important decision should be observable and auditable**
* **Reliability controls must be enforced in code**
* **Compliance should be designed into the system, not added later**

These repositories are self-contained demonstrations. Most of my current engineering work is private or closed-source.
